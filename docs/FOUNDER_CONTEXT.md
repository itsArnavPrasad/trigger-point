# Founder Context: who we are and why Trigger Point exists

Read this first in a new session. It explains the people and the problem behind the product. The spec says what to build; this says why.

## Who we are
- Two founders, living at IIT Bombay (Powai, Mumbai).
- Besides this hackathon (Crustdata), we are building an early-stage startup, **LearnTulip**: personalised AI tutors for school students.
- We are pre-revenue and in early pilots. Demo fixtures use `learntulip.example`, never our real site.

## The problem we have ourselves
We have a product and a clear idea of what to build. We do **not** know:
- **Who buys first:** schools or parents directly.
- **Which schools:** premium IB and IGCSE international schools, ICSE schools, or CBSE schools at the high end or the middle.
- **Which parents:** tier-1 families (high income, strong careers, in Powai, Bandra or Bangalore), upper-middle families, or middle-income families.

We also cannot fill our calendar:
- On 30 Oct 2026 we walked into Hiranandani Foundation School, Hiranandani Public School and Podar International School (Powai). We could not reach the principal at any of them.
- Calls with parents are not happening often enough either.

## The philosophy: we just press the trigger
We want to meet people. That is the whole point. But the founders should not do the arranging:
1. Trigger Point finds the right schools and parents, with evidence for why each one and why now.
2. It writes the outreach, gets our approval of the exact text, and books the meeting.
3. Before each meeting it gives us a **pre-meeting brief**: who we are meeting, why them, why now, the people involved, the history so far, our angle and the questions still open.
4. We join the call. After it, outcomes feed back so it learns which segment works.

So the product is built for us first. It must also be easy for any founder to point at their own website in their own Claude Code session and get the same loop.

## What this means for the work
- **Segments are experiments.** School board (IB, IGCSE, ICSE, CBSE) and parent tier are hypotheses to test with real outcomes, never assumptions to hard-code.
- **Meetings are first-class.** The Meetings page must show what is upcoming, held, missed or unconfirmed, with Join and Notes on each meeting.
- **Walk-ins are a measured failure,** not a strategy. Prefer an email introduction to the principal before any visit.
- **The truth rules still hold.** No invented facts about real schools or people. Sample and fixture data is always labelled, and learning uses only verified outcomes (see `CLAUDE.md`).
