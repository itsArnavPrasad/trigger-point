# Review a GTM Case

Review in this order:

1. **Identity:** is the canonical company/person correct and current enough for the proposed action?
2. **ICP fit:** are fit criteria supported, and are exclusions checked?
3. **Why now:** is there a dated, relevant signal? Distinguish event date from retrieval date.
4. **Why person:** is the recipient plausibly responsible for the problem? Avoid defaulting to the most senior title.
5. **Why us:** is the seller proof point approved and actually relevant? Is the pain statement a hypothesis rather than a fact?
6. **Sources:** do the links exist, match the entity, and support each factual claim?
7. **Freshness:** are signals inside the configured freshness window?
8. **Message:** one hook, one bridge, one proof point if available, one ask; no fabricated familiarity or generic filler.
9. **Policy:** check suppression, opt-out, channel permissions, approval state and duplicate actions.
10. **Decision:** `pass`, `revise`, or `reject`, with a reason for each failed check.

Do not approve an external send. This skill only reviews and recommends; approval is a separate explicit user action.
