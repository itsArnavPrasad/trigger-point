How SupportPilot works

SupportPilot drafts answers from your help center and past resolved tickets, then routes anything uncertain to a human agent with a suggested reply.

Native integrations with Zendesk, Intercom and Salesforce Service Cloud.

Every automated answer links to the help-center article it was based on, and admins can review answers before they go live.

SOC 2 Type II report available on request.

SupportPilot is a synthetic demo company used to test Trigger Point. It is not a real business.
